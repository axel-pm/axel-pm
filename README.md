### 🛠 What I'm building

**[watch-n-learn](https://github.com/axel-pm/watch-n-learn)** · Python<br>
Point it at a how-to video and it writes a reusable skill your AI coding agent can follow later, so nobody has to watch the video again. Runs entirely on your machine, no API keys.

**[ai-coach](https://github.com/axel-pm/ai-coach)** · TypeScript<br>
A browser extension that lives in your Gmail, notices the tasks where AI would actually save you time, and hands you a ready-to-paste prompt right when the work lands.

**[roadmap-raiders](https://github.com/axel-pm/roadmap-raiders)** · TypeScript · **[▶ play it](https://axel-pm.github.io/roadmap-raiders/)**<br>
A Slay the Spire-style roguelike deckbuilder for product managers. Fight Scope Creep and The HiPPO with a deck of real Lenny's Podcast guests, their cards generated from actual episode data.

---

### The throughline

Same move every time: take a raw AI capability and hide it inside something you can pick up without reading a manual. A skill an agent reads on its own. A nudge in your inbox. A game you just start playing.
